## 2. Short summary

- You can view, correct, download and delete everything from the app.

## 3. What data is saved and why

There is one personal database: the one the app creates on your device with what you type. Only what the app needs to work is saved.

| Data | What it is for | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Profile name (max. 30 characters) | To greet you on the Home screen | Optional |
| Income per pay and main currency | To calculate "You can spend today" and the alerts | Optional |
| Savings goal per quincena | To set it aside before calculating "You can spend today" | Optional |
| Pay days and quincena schedule | To split your finances into periods | Optional (there is a default schedule: days 1 and 16) |
| Transactions: amount, currency, date, type (income or expense) | To keep your accounts, totals, history and statistics | Needed to save a transaction |
| Transactions: category, free-text note (max. 60 characters) and payment method (cash, card or SINPE) | To sort and search your transactions | Optional |
| Your own categories (max. 24 characters), budgets, recurring bills and savings moves | Budgets, reminders for recurring bills and savings balance | Optional |
| Preferences: language, theme, last currency, category and payment method used, date of the last backup, skipped leftover offers, backup reminder snooze | To remember your choices and to remind you when to make a backup | Saved automatically while you use the app |

You type all of the above yourself. The app does not ask for or read your ID number, phone, email, location, contacts, bank accounts or cards.

Financial data can show your economic situation. For that reason we treat it with the care that applies to restricted-access data. Please do not type other people's data, or data you do not want stored, in the notes.

## 4. Legal basis: your consent

Law 8968 asks for your express consent, in writing (electronic is fine). You give it on the first screen of the app, before you type any data: you tick the box "I have read and accept the Privacy policy and the Terms and conditions" (it starts unticked) and tap "Accept and continue". Your device keeps which version you accepted and on what date.

You can withdraw it at any time, in the same way and for free. Withdrawal is not retroactive. To withdraw, delete your data (section 7) or stop using the app.

## 5. Where it is stored and who receives it

- On the web, data lives in a database inside your browser (IndexedDB). On an Android phone, it will be in a database inside the app.
- The app does not send your data to any server or third party. It uses no external services, not even for fonts (they are bundled in the app).
- Recipients today: none. Nobody can look at your data, except whoever uses your device.
- Backups (JSON) and exports (CSV) are downloaded by you. After you download them, you decide where to keep them and who to share them with.
- Technical note: the site that serves the app is hosted by [PROVEEDOR DE ALOJAMIENTO]. Like any website, that provider may log technical data about the visit (for example, the IP address). It does not receive what you type in the app.

## 6. What happens if you do not provide data

You can skip each step of the welcome, or the whole setup. Without income or pay days, the app cannot calculate "You can spend today" accurately. Without transactions, there are no totals or statistics. We never ask for data in order to let you in, because there is no account.

## 7. How long it is kept and how to delete it

- Data stays on your device until you delete it. We have no copy.
- To delete your data: Settings > "Clear all data". This removes transactions, budgets, recurring bills, savings, your own categories and your profile, and restores the default schedule.
- "Clear all data" also removes the saved last-used choices (currency, category and payment method), the date of the last backup, the backup reminder snooze and the data of old versions. It only keeps the language, the theme and the record of your consent (which you withdraw separately in Settings). To remove those, clear the site data in your browser (see the cookies and storage policy) or uninstall the app.
- If you clear the site data in your browser, or uninstall the app, everything is lost and we cannot recover it. Download a backup first if you want to keep it.
- A downloaded backup stays on your device until you delete it.

## 8. Security today

- Data does not leave your device.
- Only one tab can use the data at a time, so it does not get corrupted.
- The app validates what is typed (limits for amount, text and date) and checks backups before restoring them.
- The local database is not encrypted by the app. Anyone with access to your unlocked device, or to your browser profile, could see it. Protect your device with a screen lock.
- If you lose the device or switch browsers without a backup, you lose the data.

## 9. Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, withdraw your consent and consent to any transfer of it (Law 8968, arts. 5 and 7).

In the app, you can yourself:

- View: on Home, History, Statistics, Settings and the other screens.
- Correct: tap a transaction to edit it, and edit your profile, categories, budgets and recurring bills.
- Delete: remove a transaction, or use "Clear all data".
- Download: backup (JSON) and export (CSV) in Settings and History.

Because we do not hold your data, if you write to [CORREO DE CONTACTO] we can only explain how to do this in the app and answer your questions. If one day we did hold your data (section 13), we would answer your request for free and within five business days. Please give us a way to notify you in your request.

## 10. Minors

The app is for people over 18. A person under 18 should only use it with the permission and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, who accepts this policy on their behalf. We do not ask for age. If you think a minor is using the app without permission, write to us.
