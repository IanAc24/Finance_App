- Google's main page: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637
- Rules for apps, games and in-app purchases (including subscriptions): https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/15574908

Summary of what Google says (checked on: [FECHA / DATE]):

- Usually there is no refund if you share your account or payment details, or if you abuse the policy.

## 3. Direct sales (outside Google Play)

### Right of withdrawal (derecho de retracto)

Law 7472 (art. 40) gives the consumer the right of withdrawal: they may cancel the contract, without liability, within 8 days from when the contract is formed, as long as the nature of the product allows it. The regulation of that law (Decree 37899-MEIC, art. 133, amended by Decree 40703-MEIC) says this also applies to e-commerce.

Our rule:

- You may cancel your direct purchase within 8 business days of paying, without giving a reason. [TO BE CONFIRMED BY THE LAWYER: the law says "eight days" without saying whether business or calendar days. We use business days because it gives the consumer more time.]
- If you already used part of a paid service, we may deduct the time used where the law allows it and we said so before you paid. [TO BE CONFIRMED BY THE LAWYER.]
- After that period there is no withdrawal right. Still, write to us if you had a problem: we will review it in good faith, and we will always respect your rights for defects or wrong charges.

### How to request it

1. Write to [CORREO DE CONTACTO] from the same channel or email you used to buy. Withdrawal must be requested through the same channel in which you gave consent to the purchase.
2. Give your name, the date and the amount, and say you want to use the right of withdrawal.
3. We answer and confirm within 5 business days.

### Refund period and method

- We return everything you paid, by the same method you used to pay. Decree 40703-MEIC says so.
- Period to return the money: [PLAZO DE REEMBOLSO: we propose 8 calendar days from the day after your notice. The legal period is UNVERIFIED; confirm in Decree 37899-MEIC].
- Banks or processors may take a few more days to show you the money.
- We charge no fee for the refund.

## 5. Law 10946 (digital services and e-commerce)

Law 10946, "Governance of Digital Services and E-Commerce", was published in La Gaceta (Alcance 80) on 24 June 2026. According to the sources we consulted, it enters into force 12 months after publication, that is, around June 2027, and the Executive must issue its regulation. [UNVERIFIED in La Gaceta or SCIJ.] It requires informing the consumer of the conditions, period and procedure of the right of withdrawal, and it bans designs that mislead the user.

Today that law is not yet in force. When it is, we will update this policy and comply with what it requires.
